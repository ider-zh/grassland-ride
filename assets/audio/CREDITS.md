The bundled sound bank uses short excerpts, converted to mono and filtered, gain-matched, tapered and compressed into one MP3 sprite. Runtime adds spatial placement, small gain/pitch variation and weather-dependent filtering. The wind and rain beds are synthesized noise; no recorded loop drives the hoof cadence.
